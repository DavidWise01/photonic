Natural bound: A 4×4 operant address is a transfer between incoming and outgoing lanes. Under scalar-zero endpoint pinning, H and Y each route through only 4 of their 16 control cells. O, N and E route through all 16. Hence 56 of the 80 operator-address pairs appear along the current open chain.
